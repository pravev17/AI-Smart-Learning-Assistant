Aptitude Easy - Detailed Notes

1. Number System

The number system forms the foundation of aptitude. It includes natural numbers, whole numbers, integers, rational and irrational numbers. Learn divisibility rules, factors, multiples, HCF and LCM because many aptitude questions are based on these concepts.

Tips:
• Learn multiplication tables.
• Practice mental calculations.
• Remember divisibility rules for 2,3,5,9 and 11.

2. Percentages

Percentage means 'per hundred'. Formula: Percentage=(Value/Total)×100.
Applications include examination marks, discounts, profit & loss and population growth.
Example:
If a student scores 72 out of 90, percentage=(72/90)×100=80%.

3. Ratio and Proportion

A ratio compares two quantities. A proportion states two ratios are equal. Used in partnership, mixtures and scaling problems.
Example: Ratio 2:3 means for every 2 units of one quantity there are 3 units of another.

4. Average

Average = Sum of observations / Number of observations.
Useful in marks, salaries and statistics.
Shortcut: If all values increase by the same number, the average also increases by that number.

5. Profit, Loss and Discount

Profit = SP−CP
Loss = CP−SP
Profit%=(Profit/CP)×100
Discount = Marked Price−Selling Price.
Practice shopkeeper-based problems.

6. Simple Interest

SI=(P×R×T)/100.
Understand Principal, Rate and Time. Solve daily-life banking examples.

7. Preparation Tips

Practice 15–20 questions daily. Improve speed using mental maths. Analyse mistakes after every mock test.

8. Practice & Interview

Practice 30 beginner questions. Focus on accuracy before speed.
